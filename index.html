Scientific ML in practice

A few representative projects showing how I frame problems, build models, evaluate generalization, and connect predictions to action.

01
Industry · Battery R&D · Lab-in-the-loop

Closed-loop design of battery electrolytes

Connected a mixture-aware property model with constrained multi-objective optimization to propose electrolyte formulations that balance ionic conductivity, viscosity, and flash point, then shortlisted diverse candidates from across the Pareto front for laboratory testing.

Predict→Optimize→Test in the lab→Refine ↺

The lab then reshaped the problem. When some formulations would not mix during preparation, I located the failing composition region and revised the optimizer's composition bounds. When measurements showed a systematic viscosity overprediction in concentrated electrolytes, we flagged that regime as unreliable for optimization and targeted it for new training data.

mixture-aware GNN surrogate NSGA-III Pareto search lab-informed constraints model-reliability checks

02
Industry · Battery R&D · Experiment prioritization

Learning to rank electrolyte formulations by cycle life

Cycling tests are slow and lab capacity is limited, so the real question was which formulation to test next. Across 1,400+ cycling experiments, I described each formulation with structural, molecular-DFT, and surface-DFT descriptors aggregated by composition, kept only features that were stable across folds, and trained a listwise ranker within comparable cycling protocols.

To avoid optimistic estimates, evaluation used nested grouped cross-validation that held out entire component combinations, with all feature selection and tuning inside the training folds.

1,400+ experiments mixture representations XGBoost listwise ranking nested grouped CV NDCG@10 + Spearman

Research · Catalysis · Active learning

Physics-informed graph learning for catalyst discovery

Used a theory-infused graph neural network that predicts physically meaningful d-band and adsorbate-orbital parameters and computes adsorption energy through a differentiable Newns–Anderson chemisorption model, so the physics sits inside the forward pass and the learned quantities can be inspected.

Wrapped the model in an active-learning loop with DFT, where each round selected the next calculations, then screened about 17,700 candidate surfaces down to 155 for DFT validation.

04
Scientific Screening · Thermodynamics

Physics-constrained screening across chemical space

Predicted critical temperature, critical pressure, and acentric factor from molecular descriptors, then fed them into Peng–Robinson equation-of-state and power-cycle calculations with engineering constraints. Cheap ML ran on everything; expensive physics ran only on the survivors, taking hundreds of millions of candidate molecules down to a small engineering review set.

large-scale screening similarity-aware validation equation of state physics constraints

Molecular ML · Generalization

Testing whether molecular models actually generalize

Built reproducible molecular-property benchmarks with chemistry-aware similarity splits and compared descriptor-based models against pretrained molecular representations. In a critical-temperature benchmark, the classical descriptor baseline remained the stronger model under the chosen generalization test.
