how i work

From chemistry to decisions

The thread across my work is not a particular model architecture. It is building an end-to-end path from scientific data to a defensible experimental or engineering decision.

01

Represent

Molecular descriptors, graph representations, DFT-derived features, embeddings, and mixture-level statistics.

→

Learn

Regression, learning-to-rank, graph ML, interpretable models, and strong classical baselines.

→
03

Validate

Similarity-aware splits, task-aligned metrics, diagnostics, and checks against realistic distribution shift.

→
04

Decide

Candidate ranking, constrained screening, multi-objective optimization, and experiment prioritization.

selected work

Scientific ML in practice

A few representative projects showing how I frame problems, build models, evaluate generalization, and connect predictions to action.

01
Industry · Battery R&D

Learning to rank electrolyte formulations

Built a learning-to-rank workflow across 1,400+ battery experiments. Molecular and physics-derived component features were aggregated into composition-aware mixture representations, then used to prioritize formulations for experimental follow-up.

1,400+ experiments mixture representations learning to rank NDCG + Spearman
Private industry case study · public-facing summary
02
Molecular ML · Generalization

Testing whether molecular models actually generalize

Built reproducible molecular-property benchmarks with chemistry-aware similarity splits and compared descriptor-based models against pretrained molecular representations. In a critical-temperature benchmark, the classical descriptor baseline remained the stronger model under the chosen generalization test.

similarity-aware CV RDKit / 2D descriptors pretrained embeddings XGBoost

Scientific Screening · Thermodynamics

Physics-constrained screening across chemical space

Combined ML-predicted thermophysical properties with equation-of-state calculations and operating constraints to screen hundreds of millions of candidate molecules down to a small engineering review set.

large-scale screening property prediction equation of state physics constraints
Private industry case study · sanitized description

Research · Catalysis

Interpretable ML for catalyst discovery

Connected DFT-derived structure and chemical-bonding information to catalytic behavior using interpretable machine learning and graph neural networks, with the goal of extracting design principles rather than treating prediction as the endpoint.
