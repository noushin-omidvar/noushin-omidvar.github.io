Composition-aggregated molecular and DFT descriptors with a listwise ranker to decide which formulation to test next.

Research · Electrocatalysis

Physics-informed graph learning for catalyst discovery

A GNN with Newns–Anderson chemisorption theory inside the model, in an active-learning loop with DFT.

Result~17,700 Pt-monolayer surfaces narrowed to 155 for DFT; known active catalysts recovered and new candidates proposed.
